Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

ScheduleBuddy is built so that your recordings and your schedule stay on your phone. This page explains exactly what the app does with your information, in plain language.

The short version

  • There is no account, and we run no server that receives your data.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own. There are no analytics, no advertising and no trackers.
  • Speech is turned into text on your phone, and commitments are found on your phone.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information, because we never receive it.

What the app uses, and why

  • Microphone. Used only between the moment you tap Start and the moment you tap Stop, to hear what is said. A timer stays on your lock screen while it is on. It never starts on its own.
  • Speech recognition and on-device language model. Audio is held in memory for a few seconds, turned into text on your phone, and then discarded. It is not written to disk.
  • Transcripts. The text of what was heard is stored in the app on your phone so each calendar item can show the sentence it came from. Transcripts expire after 30 days, and you can delete them sooner.
  • Calendar. With your permission, the app adds, shows and removes events in the calendars on your phone, including Google or Outlook calendars you have added in iOS Settings. Those calendars are managed by their own providers under their own policies.
  • Audio clips (Pro, optional). If you turn on “Hear the moment”, a 10-second clip behind a receipt can be kept so you can replay it. It is off by default, stored only on your phone, and deleted after 30 days.
  • Projects, people, places and amounts. Details the app finds, such as who is involved, where and how much, are stored only in the app on your phone.

Purchases

If you buy Pro or the lifetime unlock, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to that transaction.

Your control

  • Pause a recording, delete the last five minutes, or delete everything from inside the app.
  • Remove any calendar item in one tap.
  • Withdraw microphone, speech or calendar access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes the data it stored on your phone.

Recording other people

Laws and house rules about recording conversations differ by place and by room, and many places require everyone’s consent. You are responsible for getting permission before you record other people.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as visitors’ IP addresses, under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

Children

ScheduleBuddy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, because the app does not send information to us.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and say so in the app.
