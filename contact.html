Helpful to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if you can. Please leave out anything private.

Quick answers

  • It added something wrong. Open the event, read the sentence it came from, and tap Remove. It comes off your calendar instantly.
  • Delete my data. Use the delete option in the app’s Settings. There is no account and nothing is stored on our side.
  • Which phones work? iPhones on iOS 26 that support Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro and newer). Others get transcription and the Review inbox, but less accurate detection.
  • Android? Not yet.
